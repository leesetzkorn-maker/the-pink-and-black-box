Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the boxes?

Contents are curated by Monique according to your chosen budget and occasion. The Biltong Box can include biltong, droëwors, chilli bites, nuts and salty snacks. Other boxes can include personalised gifts, treats, keepsakes and self-care items.

Can I choose the exact items?

The box is curated by Monique. You choose the box type, budget and occasion, and you can add preferences in the message.

How much is delivery?

The Courier Guy delivery is a flat extra R100. Choose a locker/pickup location or provide a full delivery address.

How do I pay?

Orders can be paid by EFT using the bank details shown at checkout. Always check the order reference and account details before paying.
