# 🎀 Product Photo Upload Folders

Upload product photos into these folders:

- `pink-black-box/` — The Pink & Black Box
- `biltong-box/` — Biltong, droëwors, chilli bites, nuts and salty snacks
- `birthday/` — Birthday gift examples
- `wedding-anniversary/` — Wedding and anniversary gifts
- `get-well-support/` — Get well and support gifts
- `self-care/` — Self-care gifts

## How to upload
1. Open the folder you want.
2. Click **Add file → Upload files**.
3. Upload the photos.
4. Commit the changes to `main`.

The website can then be updated to display the uploaded photos in the matching product gallery.

**Tip:** Keep individual images reasonably sized (ideally under 5–10 MB) so browser uploads do not fail.
