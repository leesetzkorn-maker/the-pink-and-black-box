Worldwide families

When your Purrrfect match lives across the world.

Purrrfect Cats can present an international relocation pathway for families outside South Africa. The exact process depends on the destination, the individual cat, veterinary requirements and airline or transport rules.

01

Initial enquiry

Tell us your country, city, household and preferred timing. We first establish whether the cat and destination may be a practical fit.

02

Destination planning

Import rules differ between countries and can change. Destination-specific requirements should be checked with the relevant official authorities and transport provider.

03

Veterinary preparation

Depending on destination, preparation may involve identification, vaccinations, veterinary certificates, tests or permits. Requirements are confirmed case by case.

04

Documents & timing

A travel plan can be built around the required documentation, waiting periods and appointments. Do not rely on a generic timeline for every country.

05

Travel planning

Crate, airline or specialist transport arrangements and handover details are planned around the destination and approved travel route.

06

Individual quotation

International costs vary significantly by destination and circumstances. Any relocation, veterinary and transport costs should be quoted individually.

International FAQ

Do you ship worldwide?

This proposal is designed to support worldwide enquiries and relocation planning. Actual availability and transport feasibility must be confirmed for the individual cat and destination.

Will every country have the same requirements?

No. Import, veterinary, quarantine, documentation and airline requirements can differ by destination and can change over time.

How much will international relocation cost?

There is no responsible single worldwide price. Costs depend on destination, route, veterinary preparation, documents, crate and transport arrangements.

Can I choose a cat before contacting the cattery?

Yes. The site is designed so you can explore individual cat profiles and use Purrrfect Match before submitting an enquiry.
