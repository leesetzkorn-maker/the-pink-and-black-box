03 · International families

From South Africa to your home, worldwide.

Purrrfect Cats can present an international relocation pathway directly on the website so overseas families know what happens next. Each destination is different, so the final plan must be confirmed for the buyer's country and the individual cat.

Typical planning areas

  • Destination-country import requirements
  • Veterinary examinations and required documents
  • Microchip, vaccinations and health records where required
  • Airline and travel-crate requirements

Transparent communication

  • Estimated timeline before travel
  • Relocation/shipping costs quoted separately
  • Documents and milestones shown clearly
  • Buyer receives a dedicated checklist before travel

Important: International relocation requirements can change by destination. The final website should never promise a route or timeframe until the destination requirements and veterinary/export arrangements have been confirmed.
